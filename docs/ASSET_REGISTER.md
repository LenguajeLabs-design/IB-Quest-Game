## Generated map constraints

The map prompt required original characters, geography, buildings, and composition. It expressly prohibited words, logos, official marks, trademarked symbols, watermarks, and copying the supplied reference characters or layouts. Live interface text and controls are rendered in HTML rather than baked into the image.

Before public release, the accountable product or legal owner must record the approved use, owner, retention policy for source files, and any required attribution.
